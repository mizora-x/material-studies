Material Studies

Toggles.

Small single-page studies in material and motion: raw WebGL2, Web Audio and a hand-stamped pixel buffer, no libraries. Each switch tells a little story as you drag it.

  1. No. 062Daybreak Switch.Night → MorningA jelly-glass knob drags the morning across a cosy pixel room. A cat wakes, the bulbs light up, and someone knocks at the door.
  2. No. 063Thaw Switch.Winter → SpringA drop of glass carries spring through a snowed-in hollow. The ice cracks, a bear cub wakes up, and geese come home to the pond.
Sound starts on first touch. Best with headphones.
